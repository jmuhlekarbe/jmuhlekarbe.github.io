# Biography

Johannes Muhle-Karbe is the Head of the Mathematical Finance Section and a Chair in [Mathematical Finance](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/mathematical-finance/) in the [Department of Mathematics](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/mathematics) at [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/).

Johannes’ research spans Stochastic Optimization, Applied Probability and Applications in Finance.

To drive interdisciplinary research, Johannes founded and co-leads the [Imperial Centre of Excellence in Quantitative Finance](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/quantitative-finance-centre/), a college-wide research hub. He is also the Director of the [CFM-Imperial Institute of Quantitative Finance](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/quantitative-finance/). He leads research cooperations with leading hedge funds and banks such as Capital Fund Management, Deutsche Bank, Nomura, Qube Research and Technologies.

Prior to joining Imperial College, Johannes Muhle-Karbe was an Associate Professor in the Department of Mathematical Sciences at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/), and an Associate Professor at the [University of Michigan](https://umich.edu/). Before that he was an Assistant Professor at [ETH Zürich](https://www.ethz.ch/) and a Member of the [Swiss Finance Institute](https://industry.sfi.ch/en/).

Johannes currently serves as an Associate Editor for Applied Mathematical Finance, Finance and Stochastics, Frontiers in Mathematical Finance, the International Journal of Theoretical and Applied Finance, Mathematics and Financial Economics, and Quantitative Finance.

His PhD thesis at [Technische Universität München](https://www.tum.de/en/) was awarded the Förderpreis of the Fachgruppe Stochastik of the German Mathematical Society in 2010, and the inaugural Nicola Bruti Liberati Prize of the Bachelier Finance Society in 2012.
